Wanderkin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

Wanderkin (package com.tyramiso.wanderkin) is a game where the steps you walk hatch eggs and power your creatures. This policy explains what the app reads, why, and where it goes.

What we read

  • Your step count, and only that. On Android the app asks for the Health Connect permission android.permission.health.READ_STEPS; on iOS (later) it will ask for read access to Apple Health step count.
  • Wanderkin never writes health data and reads nothing else from Health Connect or Apple Health.
  • Your game progress (creatures, items, settings, step totals) is saved by the game itself.

Why

Steps are the game's main currency: walking hatches eggs and rewards you. We use step data for nothing else.

Where it is stored

Step history and your save game are stored only on your device as a local file. Nothing is sent to any server. Wanderkin has no accounts.

Ads, purchases and analytics (planned)

Wanderkin may add optional rewarded ads and in-app purchases through the app stores' own billing and an ad provider's SDK, plus basic event analytics (for example "a purchase completed" or "a level was cleared"). These are not in the app yet; when they ship this page will name the providers. Analytics will only ever record game events, never step counts or any health data. Ad and purchase providers handle their own data under their own privacy policies (for example the ad provider's policy and Google Play or the App Store).

Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone. If ads, purchases or analytics are added in future, your health data (steps) will never be passed to them; it stays on your device.

Feedback you choose to send

An optional comment tool (hidden in store builds unless you switch it on in Settings) saves a screenshot and a short activity log on your device and opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you pick an app and send it yourself. Step counts are left out of the log.

Children

Wanderkin is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

Your choices

  • Turn off step access at any time in Health Connect settings (Android) or Health settings (iOS). The game keeps working without new steps.
  • Delete all your data by uninstalling the app, or by using Reset save in the in-game Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for meaningful changes, tell you in the app.
