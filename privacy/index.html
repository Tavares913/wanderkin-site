Wanderkin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

Wanderkin (package com.tyramiso.wanderkin) is a game where the steps you walk hatch eggs and power your creatures. This policy explains what the app reads, why, and where it goes.

What we read

Your step count, and only that, from your phone's health store. On Android the app asks for the Health Connect permission android.permission.health.READ_STEPS; on iOS (later) it will ask for read access to Apple Health step count. Wanderkin never writes health data and reads nothing else from Health Connect or Apple Health.

Why

Steps are the game's main currency: walking hatches eggs and rewards you. We use step data for nothing else.

What stays on your device

Your step history (the day-by-day counts the app reads) stays on your device and is never uploaded, shared or sold.

Cloud save

So you can keep your game on a new phone, Wanderkin backs up your save game with Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication and Cloud Save, run by Unity Technologies). When you first open the game it creates an anonymous player id (no name, email or phone number). Your save game (creatures, items, settings, and game totals such as how far you have travelled in the game) is stored against that id. The save holds game totals only, never your step history. Unity processes this data for us under its own privacy policy (unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

Ads

Wanderkin shows optional rewarded video ads (you choose to watch one, for example to hatch an egg early). Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising id, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent first through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform); if you decline, ads are not personalised. Google handles this data under its own policy (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Your step count and any health data are never passed to the ad SDK. You can reset or delete your advertising id in your phone's settings.

Purchases and analytics (planned)

In-app purchases will go through the app stores' own billing (Google Play or the App Store). Basic event analytics (for example "a level was cleared") may be added; they will only ever record game events, never step counts or any health data. When either ships, this page will name the provider.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Beyond the service providers named above (Unity for cloud save, Google for ads), we do not share it with anyone. Health data is never shared with anyone.

Feedback you choose to send

An optional comment tool (hidden in store builds unless you switch it on in Settings) saves a screenshot and a short activity log on your device and opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you pick an app and send it yourself. Step counts are left out of the log.

Children

Wanderkin is a general-audience game, not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn off step access at any time in Health Connect settings (Android) or Health settings (iOS). The game keeps working without new steps.
  • Delete your cloud save and anonymous account in the game: Settings > Account > Delete. This removes the cloud copy; the game on your phone stays.
  • Delete the data on your phone by uninstalling the app, or with Reset save in the in-game Settings.
  • You can also ask us to delete your cloud data by email (below).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for meaningful changes, tell you in the app.
